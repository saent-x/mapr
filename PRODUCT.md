# MAPR Product Context

## Register
product

## Product Purpose
MAPR is a live OSINT watchdesk for monitoring geopolitical and operational risk. It turns continuously ingested, source-grounded news and social signals into map-first intelligence workflows: watchlists, alerts, briefs, dossiers, source packs, and cases.

## Primary Users
- Independent analysts who need fast situational awareness without building their own ingestion stack.
- Risk, security, logistics, NGO, and research teams monitoring regions, entities, or topics.
- Journalists and investigators who need citations, source packs, and temporal change tracking.

## Positioning
MAPR is not a generic AI chat app. ChatGPT answers a question. MAPR watches live data, detects change, preserves context, cites sources, and helps an analyst produce work product.

## Product Principles
- Map first: the main working surface is the mapper page.
- Source first: every claim traces back to stored live-source evidence.
- Computed facts, generated prose: counts, deltas, charts, and confidence are deterministic.
- Free proves trust; Pro sells persistence, automation, exports, and deeper work product.
- Social is useful but always visibly unverified.

## Anti-Patterns
- Do not sell quota as the product.
- Do not make the map decorative behind a chat app.
- Do not hide core workflows on separate dashboard pages.
- Do not use model-authored HTML.
- Do not make upgrade prompts generic SaaS cards detached from the analyst workflow.
